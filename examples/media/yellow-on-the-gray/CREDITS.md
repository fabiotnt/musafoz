# Yellow on the Gray

An animation based on a concept by F&aacute;bio Nicolau de Lima about bringing dead
urban spaces back to life. A capuchin jumps across a gray city, turning each
building yellow when it lands.

- Concept and attribution: F&aacute;bio Nicolau de Lima, 2026.
- Video generation: Google Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash. The generated audio track
  was removed for this release.
- Source: [Yellow on the Gray (MP4)](yellow-on-the-gray.mp4).
- Vertical edition: [Yellow on the Gray - Vertical (MP4)](yellow-on-the-gray-vertical.mp4).
  Reframed from the landscape edition to 9:16 using a moving crop.
